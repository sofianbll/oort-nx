Help

Straight answers.

What is Oort NX?

A launcher: one library for your games, made for the controller. It arranges them, starts them and counts the time. It contains no games.

Is it free?

Yes, for life. The code is open under the MIT license, no feature is paid and none is locked.

Does it provide games, keys or firmware?

No, and it does not help find any. Compiling a Switch game needs your own files and your own keys, and the right to use them.

macOS or Windows warns me. Is that normal?

Yes, releases are not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. Only download from this site, compare the checksum, then follow the Install page.

I come from 0.3.0. Do I lose anything?

No. On first launch your library is copied and checked. The old one stays intact and 0.3.0 still opens it.

My controller is not recognized

Plug it in before opening Oort, then look at Settings › Controllers: every button should light up. If not, tell us the model in a report.

How do I uninstall?

Move the app to the Trash. Your games and saves stay put.

I found a problem

Tell us on GitHub: what you were doing, what you saw, your version. For a vulnerability, use private reporting.
