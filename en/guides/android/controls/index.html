Controller and touch

Android0.1.0-alpha.14 steps3 min

  1. 1

    By touch

    Everything works by touch. Every target is at least 48 dp.

    Checked with invented games : Where this comes from
    • android/tools/emulator_check.py
  2. 2

    With a controller

    D-pad or sticks to move. A confirms, B goes back, X sorts or opens the page, Y opens the controllers. Start opens the settings, L1 and R1 change tab.

    Checked with invented games : Where this comes from
    • android/tools/emulator_check.py
    • android/app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/sofianbll/oortnx/core/PadKeys.kt
  3. 3

    Try your buttons

    Controllers › Try the buttons: each button lights up. Hold A or B to close. Checked with a simulated controller, not a real one.

    Checked with invented games : Where this comes from
    • android/tools/emulator_check.py
    • android/app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/sofianbll/oortnx/core/PadMonitor.kt
  4. 4

    Swap A and B

    For a controller with the Switch layout: Controllers › Swap A and B.

    Checked with invented games : Where this comes from
    • android/tools/emulator_check.py
    • android/app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/sofianbll/oortnx/core/Prefs.kt
What has been tried

Tried in an emulator (Android 10, 14 and 16) with invented games. Never on a real phone.
