Play and come back

Android0.1.0-alpha.13 steps2 min

This guide describes 0.1.0-alpha.1. 0.1.0-alpha.2 is out since. What changes

  1. 1

    Start

    Tap Play. Android opens the game’s app.

    Checked with invented games : Where this comes from
    • android/tools/emulator_check.py
    • android/app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/sofianbll/oortnx/core/GameLauncher.kt
  2. 2

    While playing

    The Back gesture, or the controller’s Menu button, opens the game’s pause menu. Its settings and saves are there.

    Read in the code, not tried : Where this comes from
    • android/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml
    • android/README.md
  3. 3

    Come back

    Quit the game or use the recent apps. Oort notes the day it was started, not the play time.

    Checked with invented games : Where this comes from
    • android/tools/emulator_check.py
    • android/app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/sofianbll/oortnx/core/LastStart.kt
What has been tried

Tried in an emulator (Android 10, 14 and 16) with invented games. Never on a real phone.
