0.5.0-beta.4

Latest

November 26, 2026Mac8 changes

Find what you want to do

Find a game, a setting or an action in one place. If you remove a favorite by mistake, you can undo it.

New

  • Search in one place

    Press ⌘K to find a game, a setting or an action. With a controller, open Find a game, then press Y.

  • Undo a removal

    You can put back a favorite or a Mac shortcut you just removed.

  • Copy a game’s settings

    See what will change before you copy settings to another game. You can also reset a single setting.

Better

  • Refresh everything together

    Refresh your games and online library at once. Activity shows what changed and what Oort couldn’t read.

  • Choose your Google Drive folders

    Setup helps you choose where to keep your games and their files. When you sign out, you can keep your Google ID and secret on this Mac. Your files stay on Drive.

  • Use the controls you prefer

    Switch between mouse, controller and keyboard. Right-click for options, and the buttons stay available if you hide the hints.

  • Choose where to see player counts

    In Settings › Multiplayer, choose where to show Nextendo’s player counts. Turn off all four options to stop looking up the counts.

Fixed

  • Online game archives

    Oort no longer reports a failure when a transfer finishes but its response arrives late.

Good to know

Try Frost, Mist or Nucleus in Settings › Sound. Your current choice stays the same. You can support Oort NX on Ko-fi in Settings › About; the Arcane credits are still there.

Coming from 0.3.0

Coming from 0.3.0? Install the DMG once. Oort copies your library and leaves the old one in place.

Finish any pending removals and installs in the old version. Don’t run both versions at once.

Not tried yet

This beta hasn’t been tried with a real controller, a real Google Drive transfer, a real game or on another Mac. Keep a copy of the data that matters to you.

Install

You need an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later. Apple hasn’t checked this app. If macOS blocks the first launch, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. You don’t need to rebuild your games.
