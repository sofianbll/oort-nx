0.1.0-alpha.2

November 26, 2026Android5 changes

Find your games again

Search your library, keep your favourite games close and pick up where you left off.

New

  • Search and favourites

    Press Y on your controller to search. Open a game’s options with + or a long press to add it to your favourites.

Better

  • Controller, keyboard and mouse

    Hints show your controller’s buttons. Use Tab and Enter on the keyboard, or right-click a game to open its options.

  • Small window, large text

    Play stays visible in a short window. Hints wrap onto a second line when needed.

  • Same wording as on Mac

    Oort uses the same labels as on Mac and shows the full credits.

Fixed

  • Back to Oort

    Back from a game’s Android page returns you to Oort. You keep your place even if Android recreates or closes the app.

Good to know

Oort launches nx2android games already installed. It includes no games, keys or engine.

Uninstalling a game deletes its saves: export them first.

No cloud yet.

Not tried yet

194 checks in the emulator (Android 10, 14 and 16) with made-up games. Not tried on a real phone, with a real nx2android game, a real controller or TalkBack.

Install

It installs over alpha.1. You keep your settings.

Android 10 or later. The alpha uses a provisional signing key: the final release will install alongside it.
