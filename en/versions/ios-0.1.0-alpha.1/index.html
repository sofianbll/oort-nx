November 25, 2026iPhone and iPad6 changes

Oort on iPhone and iPad

The first trial version, to sign yourself. The screens are there; we have not compiled any game for iPhone yet.

New

  • Featured

    A large cover to swipe, Play under the thumb.

  • iPhone on its side

    Two panes, and full screen with a controller.

  • iPad

    Tabs at the top, a two-pane game page.

  • Controllers in dots

    Try your buttons and set them, for every game or just one.

  • Options per game

    Picture, data, saves, cheats and shortcut.

  • Offline

    No account, nothing is collected.

Good to know

The app loads games compiled as .framework and signed with your Apple account. Oort on Mac cannot make them yet.

The part that reads and starts games is adapted from NX2Apple (MIT license). Thanks to its authors.

There is no game, no key and no engine in the app.

Not tried yet

Tried in a simulator (iOS 27.2, iPhone and iPad), with made-up games. Not tried: a real device, signing with AltStore or SideStore, iOS 26, a real game, a real controller, sound, VoiceOver, battery.

Install

iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. The IPA is not signed: add it with AltStore or SideStore and your Apple account. A free account asks you to sign again every seven days.
