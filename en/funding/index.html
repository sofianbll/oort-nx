The rules

What do I unlock?

Nothing. Oort stays whole and free for everyone, for life. No early build, no role, no badge, no bought vote.

Who picks the brick?

You, if you write #domaine or #apple in your message. Otherwise, the order of the page: the domain, then Apple. This year first, next year after.

What if it is too much?

The rest goes to next year, one year only. When everything is full, the button goes away. If a euro is left, it waits in plain sight: it pays for nothing that is not written here first.

What about fees?

The platform and the bank take their share. It is written on each gift: dots count what really arrives.

Does my name show?

No, unless you write #nom or #nom=Nickname. Otherwise: Anonymous. The ledger keeps the date, the amount and the brick. Never your email or message.

How do I check?

Every expense has its receipt. The ledger is a public file: each change stays in its history. Accounts are recomputed for each page; if they do not add up, it is not built.

What if a gift must be returned?

It leaves the bricks and stays marked “returned” in the file. If Oort stops, what is in hand is shown and returned as long as the platform allows.
