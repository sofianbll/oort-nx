Privacy

Nothing is collected. Here is everything that leaves your device.

This site

No cookies, no analytics, no font or image from elsewhere. It is hosted by GitHub, which sees your IP address as any host does. Your sound settings stay in your browser.

The app, by itself

It may ask GitHub whether a newer release exists, and look for pictures and information about your games from the services you leave on.

The app, if you ask

Google Drive, online profiles, mods and other services only connect if you turn them on, with your own account. You can unplug them.

If you give

Payment happens on the platform, never in Oort. The public ledger keeps the date, the amount and the brick; a name only if you ask.

Your keys

They never leave your computer.

The phone apps

The first alphas ask for no network access.
