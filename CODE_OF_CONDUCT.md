# Code of Conduct

Oort NX is a small project. We want issues, pull requests and discussions to stay useful and respectful.

## Expected

- Be specific and kind. Criticise the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say what you tried.
- Share only what is yours to share. Remove personal paths, keys, saves and private messages before you post.
- Keep discussions on the project, in the language of the thread (French or English).

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination, in any form.
- Pressure to share games, keys or credentials, or to help circumvent protection.
- Spam, unrelated promotion or repeated off-topic posts.
- Publishing someone else's private information.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer, [@sofianbll](https://github.com/sofianbll), through GitHub's report tools. Do not answer it in a public thread. For a security problem, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may hide a comment, lock a thread or remove a contributor. Moderation decisions are made case by case and explained when it is safe to do so.
