# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

People who play their own games on a Mac, often from a sofa with a controller, sometimes at a desk with the keyboard and mouse. They range from casual players who only want to press Play to experts who tune an engine per game. Nobody picks a level: the first screen serves the casual player, and depth opens on request.

## Product Purpose

Oort NX gathers a player's games in one library and starts them: Switch games built with nxnative, Steam games, Mac apps. It prepares a game, keeps its saves, and sets players and controllers. Success is a game found and started in seconds, and a library never lost.

## Positioning

A fork of Arcane (Bium23) with the Selene interface (Matthew Silva). It improves what is there and removes nothing. Every service is an optional integration.

## Operating Context

Full screen or windowed, 1080p to 4K, at two to three metres or at arm's length. Dark rooms. Controller first; keyboard and mouse always complete. Seven languages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Open source for good, no locked feature, fully local, no data collected.
- No game, key or firmware is provided or downloaded.
- Engine-agnostic: an engine works without Oort, and Oort works without any one engine.
- A library is copied and verified before anything new uses it; the original stays as the way back.

## Brand Commitments

- The Oort cloud, cut open, is the identity. Its halftone answers focus.
- There is one Sun, the only gold (#F4CE8E).
- Covers never fill a page background by decoration; a selected game's own artwork may.
- Selene stays a complete theme. Vantablack and Vesper are palettes.
- Sounds are made by the app; they never carry meaning alone.

## Accessibility

- Every action has a controller, keyboard and pointer path.
- State is never colour alone.
- Reduce motion shows the final state with a short fade.
- Interface size from 80 to 125 %.

## Open Decisions

- A text-only size up to 200 % (Xbox accessibility guideline 101) is not built.
